# Phase 3 — Analytics dashboard

## Delivered

- Today, yesterday, rolling week, rolling month, and custom date ranges.
- Active, productive, neutral, and distracted totals.
- Category/domain rankings, hourly distribution, tab-switch counts, and productive interruptions.
- An explainable 0–100 focus score composed of productive ratio (45), distraction control (30), uninterrupted focus (15), and context stability (10).
- Responsive local dashboard with readable CSS charts; no analytics data leaves the device.

## Limitations

Goal and focus-session score factors are intentionally absent until their source modules exist. The score is deterministic and explicitly lists every included factor. Daily queries are capped at 2,000 intervals pending pagination/performance work.
